Voice & Script

DubPilot eliminates the split-attention deficit of foreign cinema. Where traditional viewing forces audiences to spend 60% of screen time reading captions at the bottom of the frame, DubPilot gives viewers a natural voice-driven conversational viewing partner built for Amazon Alexa+ and Amazon Bedrock.

A single neo-grotesque weight (400) anchors everything from 14px caption running text to 250px display letterforms. The size itself is the hierarchy. Letters collide under negative tracking to form monolithic architectural presence, while body copy breathes with generous, unhurried leading.

“Cinema is humanity’s shared visual vessel. Spoken dialogue and cultural vernacular should enrich the story—never alienate the audience or force them to look away from the frame.”

Every vocal stem—English original, Hindi, Spanish, French—is mastered under EBU R128 loudness standards. When the user speaks, Alexa+ dispatches Bedrock tool calls in sub-120ms with smart collision prevention: telemetry updates silently during video playback, speaking only when the screen is paused.
